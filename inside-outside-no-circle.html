Inside and outside, without a circle

One projection from O carries the whole unbounded outside into a bounded inside — the reciprocal t ↦ 1/t, drawn with a straightedge. No circle: the fixed point 1 is all that's left of it.

The circle's swap was d ↦ R²/d; this is the same reciprocal with R = 1 and no circle. Projection does the work, and its invariant is the cross-ratio, which needs no length. Drag the outside point toward infinity and its image sinks to the floor; the two meet only at the fixed point 1 — where the inside unit touches the number line. Where the first panel kept the circle and dropped the projection, this keeps the projection and drops the circle.
