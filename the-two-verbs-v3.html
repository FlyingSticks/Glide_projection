What this is

The classical transition piece, carried into the two-verbs apparatus. The bounding data are an n₁-gon in the picture plane (red, ratio 1) and an n₀-gon in the back plane (blue, ratio 0) — generally with different vertex counts, so no vertexwise interpolation exists. The lateral surface is built from a ladder: a cyclic monotone pairing of the two vertex rings. A rung joins a floor vertex to a picture-plane vertex; between consecutive rungs sits a panel — a warped quad where both rings advance, a flat triangle where only one does. "Quads first" is the Bresenham ladder (min(n₁,n₀) hypars, |n₁−n₀| triangles); "Triangulated" is the tinsmith's all-triangle development ladder.

What survives, what becomes data

Survives: every slice vertex is (1−t)·B + t·T for its rung, hence linear in the count; the slice is an (n₁+n₀−q)-gon whose shoelace is exactly quadratic, so V = (A₁ + 4A½ + A₀)/6 is still an identity (checked live against a 64-panel integral, and pre-build in smoke_v3.js, 7/7). The twist circuit still telescopes: ΣT⃗ = 0 over the full ladder, triangles included. A₁ and A₀ are ladder-invariant — repeated slice vertices at the collapsed ends cost the shoelace nothing.

Becomes data: the volume itself. A½, and hence V, depend on the ladder. Two fixed boundary polygons admit many transition volumes — rotate the offset and watch V move in the ledger while A₁ and A₀ hold still. This is the choice the sheet-metal chapters were really teaching: the development you cut depends on the correspondence you drew.

Three experiments

1 — Rotate the offset. A₁ and A₀ freeze; A½ and V jump. The boundary figures are vector nouns; the ladder is covector plumbing — transport data joining them, with real consequences one dimension up.

2 — Switch Quads first ↔ Triangulated. The warp census collapses toward the triangle panels' zero — flat panels carry twist in the circuit but no warp — while the circuit identity |ΣT⃗| = 0 holds through the change. Warp is a property of the panels; the circuit is a property of the ladder.

3 — Spin the glide. As in v2: V, all three areas, and the census hold at drift ≈ 1e−15. The apparatus group does not see the ladder either — pairing and gauge are independent layers of data.

Provenance

Ladder machinery smoke-tested in Node before build (smoke_v3.js, 7/7); gauge machinery inherited from v1/v2. Square-to-round and polygon-to-polygon transition pieces with triangulated developments are standard descriptive-geometry and sheet-metal material; the Bresenham ladder is the max-quad refinement of that triangulation, keeping Candela's hypar panels wherever the two rings advance together.
