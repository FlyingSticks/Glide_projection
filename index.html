Glide Projection

Interactive constructions in non-central projection — the vanishing-axis glide and cross-slit projection — for the pictorial depiction of broad, shallow space. Each sheet is a live drawing you can steer; the glide sheets take a dropped .obj.

A projection is fixed by the set its projectors pass through. These sheets work the anisotropic corner of that idea — where the aspect ratio a frontal square casts varies with depth, so shape encodes depth. Every construction is verified computationally before it is drawn.
