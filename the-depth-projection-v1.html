THE WRITE-UP

The construction. An irregular shape is laid in the picture plane, where it is seen in true size and shape. Each vertex is carried back through depth along a straight depth line, and at each depth station the whole shape reappears as a true copy — same shape, smaller by the factor (1 − r·g). To carry measure back as well, each edge is divided into equal parts, and the division is transferred to every copy. This is the Type A transfer: the fraction of the way along an edge is preserved exactly, at every depth, for any shape whatever. Nothing about the shape's irregularity is an obstacle — the law acts edge by edge and knows nothing of the whole.

What stays straight and what bends. Three families are dead straight at every setting: the edges of every copy, the depth lines of the vertices, and the depth lines of every part mark — the last all concurring at the seat, which is Type A's collector made visible. One family bends: the diagonals drawn across the wall cells. A diagonal is straight in space, but its image tracks a position that changes linearly while the scale it is drawn at also changes linearly, and the product of two linear laws is a quadratic. The bend is therefore not an artifact of drawing; it is an exact parabola, and it vanishes only when the rate is zero, or the segment stays in one depth plane, or runs along one depth line.

The toggle is the argument. Switch the same figure to one-point perspective and two things trade places. The diagonals snap dead straight — perspective is projective on space and cannot bend a line. But the depth stations, equally spaced under the glide, fall into a shrinking progression along every depth line. Each projection is exact about what the other distorts: perspective pays in spacing, the glide pays in straightness. A drawing that shows curving diagonals over equal stations, or straight diagonals over crowding stations, is telling you which instrument made it. That is what it means for the bend to be the board's signature and the progression to be the camera's.
