The census

The edges-image of a cube: a picture lattice, its depth lines, and the depth planes between them. Switch how it is drawn — both channels Type A, both Type B, or a free bend that belongs to no projection at all. The metric changes everywhere. The cross-ratio survives A and B and dies under the bend. The census — how many lines, how many points, which passes through which — survives everything. That is the floor the drafting-board constructions stand on.

House rule: no claim without a passing check. check_census.js holds the ledger (6/6): identical census and incidence table across every mode and parameter; cross-ratio of equal-ΔZ marks preserved to 10⁻¹⁵ under A and B and broken by the bend; equal ΔZ never equally spaced (the gauge is projective in Z); the graduated edge exact under A and B only. Structure and layer order follow the topology/count sheet; the placement law is the channel law of the six-core-armatures sheet.
