One condition, three names — the harmonic cut, and where it lives

The harmonic configuration is one equation, |ad| = |bc|, wearing three costumes: matched size (the two complement rectangles have equal area — Euclid I.43), similar shape (the two diagonal-rectangles have equal aspect ratio), and straight corner-diagonals (the two corner-diagonals through the inner point line up). Drag the cut and all three move together; they reach the condition at the same instant. The signed cross-ratio λ = ad/bc then splits the two states: harmonic (λ = −1, one cut outside, signed areas cancel) and coincident (λ = +1, both inside, areas add — a perfect square). This is the same harmonic event the cross-ratio page and the lateral projection carry — the hinge between them.
