The grid at left is the whole object: M = [[a, b], [c, d]], with the dashed mirror diagonal across it. Sweep the four sliders. The machine's action is drawn live: the frame arrows e₁, e₂ land on the columns — column 1 is Me₁, column 2 is Me₂ — and the grey unit circle is carried to the image oval. The parallelogram they span has signed area det = ad − bc: drive it to zero (preset provided) and watch the machine flatten the whole plane onto a line — the degeneracy alarm, P7's cone in miniature. The two off-diagonal cells b and c are reflections of each other in the mirror: when they agree, the grid equals its transpose, the machine is even-handed — (Mp)·q = p·(Mq), reciprocity in embryo — and the gold conic p·Mp = 1 stands with its perpendicular principal axes drawn. When b ≠ c the conic doesn't flinch: the quadratic form reads only the symmetric half (M+Mᵀ)/2, and the lopsided remainder is invisible to it. Drag P to steer a probe point; its image MP and the two dot products against the fixed probe q are in the readout, agreeing exactly when the mirror holds.

Everything the board asks of matrices is on this one sheet. A matrix is a recipe for moving points, known entirely by where it sends the frame — the columns. Applying it is dotting rows against coordinates; composing machines is D = C∘R. The transpose is the same machine addressed from the other slot, tied to the original by the balance law (Mp)·q = p·(Mᵀq), which holds for every grid without exception. Symmetry — the grid equal to its mirror image — is even-handedness: the machine cannot tell which point came first, and that is reciprocity, the engine of every polarity. Feed a symmetric grid the same point twice and its vanishing locus is a conic here, a quadric one dimension up: the circle matrix diag(1, 1, −R²) of the polarity primer, the ruled-surface matrix of stage C. The determinant is the degeneracy alarm — area scale of the frame image, zero exactly when the machine flattens, which is P7's cone at the perspective corner. Grids as moves, transpose as balance, symmetry as quadric, determinant as alarm: the full kit.
