The trichotomy

Four random lines, six crossings, three ways to pair the lines. Ask one question of each pairing — how does its gap line divide the other four vertices? — and the three answers are always 0 – 4, 1 – 3, 2 – 2, one of each. That is the whole classification. Drag anything; the badges re-sort themselves.

gap pair A, B  ·  remaining four by side of the gap line: near far  ·  quadrilateral simple crossed  ·  diagonal-triangle vertices — only three exist, and each lies on two of the three gap lines

House rule: no claim without a passing check. check_simple.js holds this ledger (8/8): each 4-gon's four sides lie one each on the four given lines; the three splits are always 0–4, 1–3, 2–2; the 2–2 pairing is the crossed one in 2000/2000 arrangements; and cr = −1 holds on all three regardless. Simplicity is affine, not projective — which is why it merges the 0 and 1 cases and cannot serve as the selector.
