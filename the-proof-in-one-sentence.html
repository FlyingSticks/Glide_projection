Reference Plate · Non-Constructibility

The Proof in One Sentence

A construction made only of joins and meets can't produce anything that a join-and-meet-preserving map can destroy — and the Type B fan is such a map, destroying even spacing in plain sight.

Step one — the recipe cannot tell

The panel runs a genuine straightedge recipe: from the givens A, B, C, P, F it performs five joins and four meets and outputs the point D — the harmonic conjugate, the quadrangle construction from your own sheets. Now transform the page projectively. Every given moves; every construction line moves; and the recipe, replayed from scratch on the moved givens, lands its output exactly on the moved image of the old output. The amber dot never leaves the amber ring.

That is equivariance, and the reason for it is almost too plain: the recipe is made of joins and meets, and the map preserves joins and meets, so the recipe passes through the map untouched — transform-then-construct equals construct-then-transform. The recipe has no way to notice it was transformed, because everything it can speak of is preserved. Consequence: anything a straightedge can construct from given data rides along with every projective map of that data. Constructible implies undestroyable.

Step two — one drawing refutes every procedure

Contrapositive: to prove that even spacing is not constructible, you need not examine any procedure at all. You need one join-and-meet-preserving map that destroys even spacing. The second panel is that map, and it is your drawing: the Type B fan. Five equally spaced marks ride through a finite collector onto another line. Every join is still a join, every meet still a meet — the audit in the readout finds nothing broken — and the spacing arrives as a progression, ruined. Slide the collector to infinity and the fan becomes your Type A parallels: spacing restored, which is exactly why the affine datum is a point at infinity and nothing less.

Were even spacing a creature of joins and meets, step one says no such map could touch it. The fan touches it. Therefore no straightedge procedure — tried, untried, or undreamt — ever outputs it. The graduation must be brought in from the outside: a theorem, proved by a drawing you had already made.

Why non-existence needs this shape

"There is no procedure" quantifies over every finite sequence of joins and meets, so it can never be settled by failing a few attempts. The invariance argument closes all routes at once: it finds the one property every route must have (equivariance) and exhibits one object without it (spacing, under the fan). Everything in the longer telling — homogeneous coordinates, harmonic conjugates, Playfair — was scaffolding around those two moves. The two panels are the two moves.

Step one: what the tool builds, the map carries. Step two: the map wrecks the spacing. Conclusion: the tool never built it.
