The glide axioms — four axioms, one square

The whole family, put in order: four axioms, and everything else demoted to theorem. Two frames on the drawing — a picture frame that draws true-size and a depth frame that is its translated, stretched copy — one shared station, and a projective measure. The three pins use up all three Möbius freedoms, so the law λ = k/(k+1) is not chosen: it is forced. Drag anywhere — the two frames, the aperture plate, and the light test move as one object.

Axiom S is the personality of the family. Keep the one station and you glide: the law is single, the tracks are straight, the depth frame survives, and the surface is closed to light everywhere but the corners. Split the station and you photograph: the law divides, the tracks curve, the frame collapses to a point, and a straight ray threads two slits. The square is the space of depth frames; the perimeter is where they degenerate; the corners are where light gets in.
