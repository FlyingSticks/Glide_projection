Half-formed

A slit does not dim an image or blur it. It settles one coordinate completely and leaves the other entirely free. So a half-organised image is not a faint point — it is a line, and the scene arrives at the picture plane sorted into stripes but not yet into points. Slide the picture plane and watch how many coordinates exist by the time the ray gets there.
