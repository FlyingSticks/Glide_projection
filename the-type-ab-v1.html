A channel's depth lines are the drawn edges. At r = 0 they are parallel — Kevin's Type A, no collector, ratios ride through onto anything. At r > 0 every depth line of the channel concurs at gauge 1/r — the seat (or, past the frame, the directing seat) is the collector point, and the channel is Type B: cross-ratio survives, equal parts arrive in progression on any non-parallel line. The pushbroom pairs one of each — the Hybrid panel of the drafting sheet.
